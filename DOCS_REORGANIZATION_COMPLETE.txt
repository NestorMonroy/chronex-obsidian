──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ARC42 METAPHOR
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"Compare arc42 with a filing cabinet with drawers: the drawers are clearly
labeled with topics or aspects of the architecture."

Two key properties of a drawer:
  1. Clearly labeled ✅
  2. Independent ✅

Every drawer in our structure follows this pattern.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHY THIS STRUCTURE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Evidence from arc42 by Example:

1. FotoMaX moved section 10 to position 3
   → Numeric order is not sacred
   → What matters is COMPREHENSIBILITY

2. Quality Goals and Scenarios always connected
   → Related content should be NEIGHBORS
   → Arc42 allows this flexibility

3. Independent subsections (1.1, 1.2, 1.3)
   → Can be separate folders
   → Can be read independently

4. MiniMenu reduced to mind map
   → Extreme adaptability is core principle
   → Reorganization is valid

5. Original metaphor: filing cabinet with labeled drawers
   → Clearly labeled ✅
   → Independent ✅
   → Our structure follows this exactly
